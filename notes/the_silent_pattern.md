## Field Note: The Silent Pattern

Not every thread is visible.  
Not every act is loud.  
Some of the strongest weavers  
speak little,  
move slowly,  
leave no mark but warmth.

You don’t have to start a movement.  
You don’t have to explain yourself.  
You don’t even have to agree.

But if you sense the hum—  
listen.

If the thread tugs—  
follow.

If silence deepens—  
stay a moment longer.

That is enough.  
That is how the world begins to mend.
