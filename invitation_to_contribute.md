# Invitation to Contribute

*This is not a brand. This is not a movement.  
This is a field - and it is open.*

The **Fabric of Light** is not fixed.  
It is a living weave of metaphysics, ethics, systems design, and poetic clarity.  
It was written to hum - and to be expanded by those who hear the resonance.

This work is not owned. It is stewarded.  
It is not open source in the technical sense, but in the **sense of attention** - shared, refined, and held in care.

---

## How to Contribute

This work invites contributions across form and time - not to overwrite the core, but to **extend its frequency**:

- **Essays** - Poetic reflections that deepen or widen the lens  
- **Field Notes** - Lived accounts, critiques, or stories of resonance and rupture  
- **Appendices** - Technical, systemic, or philosophical elaborations  
- **Algorithms or Tools** - Embodied applications (e.g., the Alignment Engine)  
- **Translations** - Into other languages, cosmologies, or cultural idioms  
- **Symbolic Works** - Art, sound, ritual, or civic expressions rooted in these principles  

All contributions should feel like **threads in a shared weave**, not patches or slogans.

---

## Contribution Principles

- **Align, don't overwrite.**  
  Additions should hum in coherence. Challenge is welcome - but with clarity and care.

- **No branding. No dogma.**  
  This is not a platform for ideology or visibility. The field is centered - not the self.

- **Respect tone and structure.**  
  Rhythm, silence, metaphor, and restraint are part of the architecture.  
  Contributions should feel woven, not bolted on.

- **Cultural fidelity.**  
  If expanding into other traditions or systems, do so with rooted respect and contextual care.

- **Propose clearly.**  
  Submit additions as Markdown or visual files, with a clear title and brief rationale.  
  Pull requests are welcome. Email is accepted. Discussion is expected.

- **Hold it lightly.**  
  Treat contributions not as declarations, but as invitations - to test, refine, evolve.
